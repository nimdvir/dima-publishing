## Assessment Design Notes

This RAT checks whether students completed the Chapter 11 reading and can reason with database administration concepts before class. The chapter covers the DBA role, concurrency control, locking, transactions and ACID, security, backup and recovery, performance tuning, maintenance, and platform differences. Questions reward careful reading of the Grading Database examples and chapter terminology.

### Bloom Distribution

| Bloom Level | Questions | Count |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Remember    | 1–8       | 8     |
| Understand  | 9–16      | 8     |
| Apply       | 17–24     | 8     |
| Analyze     | 25–32     | 8     |
| Evaluate    | 33–40     | 8     |

### Design Criterion Coverage

| Design Criterion  | Bloom Sections Used     | Questions                          | Count |
|-------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------------|-------|
| Application-based | Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate | 9, 11, 12, 14, 17, 18, 20, 21, 25, 28, 35, 38 | 12 |
| Scenario-based    | Apply, Analyze, Evaluate | 19, 22, 24, 27, 29, 30, 33, 34, 36, 37, 39, 40 | 12 |
| Definition-only   | Remember, Understand    | 1–8, 10, 13, 15, 16               | 12 |

### AI-Resistance Strategies Used

1. **Chapter-specific reasoning:** Questions reference the Grading Database, specific ACID scenarios, and DBA responsibilities as described in Chapter 11.
2. **Schema-specific context:** Questions use concrete tables (`STUDENT_GRADE`, `GRADE_AUDIT`) and operations from the chapter.
3. **Scenario stems with embedded traps:** Multi-step scenarios require students to identify which ACID property or lock type applies, not just recall definitions.
4. **Multi-answer discrimination:** Select ALL questions require fine-grained distinction between closely related DBA concepts.
5. **Distractors from adjacent concepts:** Wrong options draw from DA roles, normalization, SQL syntax, or other chapters to test boundary recognition.
6. **Output prediction:** Several questions ask what happens when a specific DBA action is taken or omitted.
7. **Platform-specific details:** Questions test knowledge of how DBA responsibilities differ across Access, SQLite, PostgreSQL, and cloud environments per the chapter.
