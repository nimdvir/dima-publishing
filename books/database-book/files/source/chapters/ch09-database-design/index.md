# Chapter 9: Database Design and ER Modeling

Teaches students to design databases from business requirements. Covers requirements analysis, the SDLC, entity-relationship modeling, Crow's Foot notation, relationship types, advanced ER concepts, normalization as a design check, ERD-to-table mapping, and common modeling mistakes.
