## Artifact Rubric

| Criterion                | Points | What to Check                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RBAC policy              | 15     | Three roles defined. Each role lists specific tables and operations (SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE). Receptionist restricted from financial data. Principle of least privilege followed.  |
| Backup plan              | 15     | Frequency, type, storage location, and retention period specified. RTO and RPO defined with reasonable values for a clinic. Backup drill evidence included (screenshot or description). |
| Recovery walkthrough     | 15     | Scenario clearly addressed. Affected tables correctly identified (APPOINTMENT, APPOINTMENT_SERVICE, INVOICE). Restoration steps logical. Recovery gap honestly documented.              |
| Integrity check          | 10     | Compact and Repair evidence. Relationship enforcement verified. Integrity query or PRAGMA output shown.                                                                                 |
| Performance indexes      | 15     | At least 3 indexes documented with justification. Index creation shown. Explanation of why each field was chosen.                                                                       |
| Business impact memo     | 10     | Clear, specific consequences for 4-hour and 2-day downtime. Data loss scenario addressed. Written for a non-technical clinic manager.                                                   |
| Professional quality     | 10     | PDF is organized, readable, and includes clear section headings. Screenshots are legible.                                                                                               |
| Cross-platform awareness | 10     | Access-specific steps (Compact and Repair, Design View indexing) are correct. SQLite alternatives noted where relevant.                                                                 |

**Total: 100 points**
